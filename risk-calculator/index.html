FAQ

Is it live?

Not yet. We’re building it with a small group of studios, and joining the list gets you early access and a say in what we build first.

What is the analysis based on?

AI analysis of your brief, checked against patterns from real finished projects, starting with our own studio’s. As you add your projects, it calibrates to yours.

How accurate is it?

You get ranges with the reasoning behind each, not a single number. It doesn’t replace your judgment; it makes sure nothing obvious gets missed.

What happens to our data?

Your briefs and project data stay private to your studio. Shared benchmarks use only anonymised, aggregated data, and only if you opt in.

Does it work for development projects?

It’s built first for design, brand and Webflow work. Development-heavy projects come later.
